Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ekonet ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act (DPA 2019). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your phone number, full name, and email address provided during registration.
  • Location Data: Device GPS coordinates and installation address details to route field technicians accurately to your premises for Wi-Fi setup.
  • Transaction Data: M-Pesa payment records and STK push transaction identifiers processed securely via our automated billing pipeline.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to provision high-speed Wi-Fi internet packages, manage order history, dispatch installation technicians, and provide prompt customer support.

4. Data Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—including Firebase cloud security standards and encrypted M-Pesa API tunnels—to protect your personal information.

5. Your Rights

Under Kenyan data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, reach out to us via the Support section in the app.
