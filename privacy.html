Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

BY USING THE EKONET APP AND SERVICES, YOU AGREE TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY. Please read this policy carefully to understand how we process, protect, and handle your data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Ekonet ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application, web portals, internet subscriptions, router installations, and customer support services (collectively, the "Services").

2. Definitions

  • Device: Mobile phones, computers, or tablets used to access Ekonet Services.
  • Protected Information: Personally identifiable data including name, email address, phone number, and physical installation address.
  • TPSP: Third-Party Service Providers assisting in payment processing, cloud hosting, SMS delivery, or field technical support.

3. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Details: Full name, phone number, and email address collected during sign-up.
  • Installation Address: Geographical installation coordinates and building details provided to route technician deployments.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage & Device Info: Device IP address, manufacturer, operating system version, app usage statistics, and network status checks.
  • Transaction Data: M-Pesa phone number, STK push status, and transaction reference numbers processed via secure payment pipelines.
  • Location Data: Device location captured during setup to confirm Wi-Fi coverage availability and assist field installation crews.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for lawful operational purposes, including:

  • Provisioning high-speed Wi-Fi internet connections and router configurations.
  • Processing M-Pesa payments, automated billing receipts, and account renewals.
  • Dispatching technical teams for installation, network inspection, and repair services.
  • Monitoring network health, diagnosing server issues, and preventing service outages.
  • Investigating fraudulent activities, illegal network usage, or terms of service violations.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information only under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers & Field Technicians: Shared with authorized field engineers and network support partners exclusively to complete installation or maintenance requests.
  • Payment Gateways: Transmitted via encrypted channels to payment partners (e.g., M-Pesa/Safaricom) to complete billing requests.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Disclosed to Kenyan law enforcement or government entities when required by applicable law, court order, or regulatory mandate.
  • Business Transfers: Transferred in connection with any corporate merger, restructuring, or acquisition of Ekonet operational assets.

6. Data Security

We employ standard, commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—including SSL encryption, secure API endpoints, and Firebase database access rules—to protect your Protected Information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your Protected Information for as long as your Ekonet account remains active or as necessary to provide internet services. Upon account cancellation or deletion requests, we retain minimal historical transaction logs as required by Kenyan legal and tax compliance obligations.

8. Your Rights Under Kenya DPA 2019

You reserve the right to access, rectify, request restriction of processing, or request full deletion of your personal data stored within our systems. You can update profile details directly in the app or submit inquiries to our support desk.
